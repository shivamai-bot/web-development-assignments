Welcome To BVDU,IMED

Institute of Management and Entrepreneurship Development is the Institute which is established by Bharati Vidyapeeth (Deemed to be University) Pune. The Institute is approved by AICTE, New Delhi and is affiliated to Bharati Vidyapeeth (Deemed to be University) Pune. The Institute is located in the heart of Pune city, which is known as the Oxford of the East. The Institute has been established with a vision to provide quality education in the field of Management and Entrepreneurship Development. The Institute offers various programs in Management and Entrepreneurship Devel opment. The Institute has a team of highly qualified and experienced faculty members who are dedicated to providing quality education to the students.
